# Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon — current official status and submission requirements

Research date: 2026-08-30. This note uses only pages owned by the event
organizers (`lablab.ai`) or sponsor (`alpaca.markets` and Alpaca's verified
company channels). It deliberately does not treat repository notes, community
posts, or search snippets from third parties as evidence.

## Answer first

The public organizer dashboard currently labels the event **“Live ·
Submissions open”**. It reports 23 submissions and 40 drafts, and lists
“Options Alpha Agents” as the active track. The public page gives the event
window as **28 August–4 September 2026**, but its currently rendered public
view does not show a deadline hour. Treat 4 September as the last stated date;
obtain the exact cutoff from the authenticated submission page or an organizer
before relying on a specific time.

Sources: [organizer live dashboard](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/alpaca-ai-trading-agents-hackathon/live), [organizer event page](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/alpaca-ai-trading-agents-hackathon).

## Confirmed event-specific requirements

The sponsor's official event announcement states all of the following as
“Submission requirements”:

1. one **dedicated competition paper account per email**;
2. that account's **starting balance set to USD 100,000**;
3. a **one-page write-up** covering the AI logic, risk gates, and Alpaca
   infrastructure implementation; and
4. **every strategy must include options trading**.

The same announcement says the agent must use Alpaca's Trading API and **either
the Alpaca MCP Server or Alpaca CLI**. It describes judging as based on P&L and
creativity or engagement, but gives no public weighting, P&L calculation
definition, tie-break procedure, or audit process.

Source: [Alpaca official company announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alpacamarkets/) (the company post links to the event page for the terms).

### What needs to be ready or submitted

| Item | Official basis | Required next state |
|---|---|---|
| Team enrollment | Organizer-wide participation guide | Confirm every team member is registered and belongs to the event team; LabLab says this also applies to solo participants. |
| Dedicated paper account | Alpaca announcement | Use exactly one competition-dedicated paper account for the team's email; retain proof of its identity and its USD 100,000 opening state. |
| Options-bearing strategy | Alpaca announcement; organizer live track | The submitted strategy must actually incorporate options trading. |
| Alpaca integration | Alpaca announcement; organizer event page | Be able to demonstrate Alpaca Trading API use plus either MCP Server or CLI use. |
| One-page write-up | Alpaca announcement | Prepare a single page that explicitly covers AI decision logic, deterministic/other risk gates, and Alpaca infrastructure. |
| Project submission | Organizer's generic submission guidance | Have a working prototype URL, public GitHub repository, MP4 pitch video of at most five minutes, and PDF slide deck ready for the platform form. |

The last row is **organizer-wide guidance**, not a retrievable event-specific
form schema on 2026-08-30. It is therefore a prudent deliverable checklist, not
evidence that every field is mandatory for this particular form.

Source: [lablab.ai guide — complete submission](https://lablab.ai/guide/ai-hackathons).

## Practical sequence for the next market session

This is an operational checklist inferred from the confirmed rules, not an
additional organizer rule:

1. Before trading, verify that the configured account is the single dedicated
   competition paper account and preserve the USD 100,000-start evidence.
2. Keep every enabled strategy options-bearing and ensure the observable
   execution path demonstrates the Trading API plus the selected MCP-or-CLI
   integration.
3. Preserve auditable trade/P&L/risk-gate evidence throughout the competition;
   it supports the required one-page explanation and any later organizer
   clarification or review.
4. Before submitting, reconcile the platform form against the one-page
   write-up, public repository, reachable app URL, video, and slides. Confirm
   exact deadline and form constraints in the authenticated organizer view.
