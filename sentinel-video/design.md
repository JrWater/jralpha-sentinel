## Overview

Sentinel should feel like a careful researcher explaining a real system in a
well-used notebook: warm enough to invite attention, precise enough to earn
trust. The whole composition uses a graphite-and-dilute-watercolor sketch
language: uneven pencil borders, paper grain, muted fill washes, and hand-drawn
annotations. Crisp typography and real evidence carry every factual claim.

## Components

- Cards use slightly irregular corners, a visible ink border, and an offset
  shadow that reads like layered paper.
- Notes, stamps, circles, and pencil lines are decorative annotations. They may
  restate an existing claim but never introduce a new metric.
- Ambient paper marks should evolve throughout a held shot. Scene 01 is a
  miniature visual explanation: orbiting robot, a note about unattended
  operation, then a hand-drawn callout around the deterministic gate matrix.
- The small Sentinel robot appears only at the opening and close. It represents
  unattended operation, not the language model itself.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep dashboards, code, account identifiers, limits, and evidence crisp.
- Do use warm paper accents sparingly to guide the eye.
- Do keep motion deterministic and synchronized to narration.
- Do use sketch fills only behind evidence, never as a filter over it.
- Don't turn financial evidence into handwriting.
- Don't use confetti, excessive bouncing, or childlike character acting.
