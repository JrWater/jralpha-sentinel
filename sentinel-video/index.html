The failure mode of an L-L-M trader
isn't being wrong --
it's being confidently wrong.
Most agents this week give the model
a broker tool and let it call
it directly. Sentinel splits that apart:
the model emits a proposal,
with no broker credentials and no submission
tool. Sixteen deterministic gates decide whether
it becomes an order.

Every position is defined-risk --
no naked shorts,
no market orders.
Two thousand dollars is the max on
any single trade,
a fraction of starting equity,
so a drawdown doesn't quietly stay just
as aggressive. Thirteen thousand across the whole
book. A daily kill switch at negative
three thousand halts new entries.
And below ninety-two thousand,
the agent enters Entry Maintenance --
no new exposure,
but exits keep running.
